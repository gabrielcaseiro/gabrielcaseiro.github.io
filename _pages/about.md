I am a PhD student in Politics at [NYU](https://as.nyu.edu/departments/politics.html) and hold an MSc in Economics from the [São Paulo School of Economics](https://economics-sp.fgv.br). My research is in comparative politics, focusing on distributive politics, electoral behavior, and electoral geography, with an emphasis on Brazil.
